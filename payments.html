Secure booking payments

Your journey,
your way to pay.

Local and international payment options are being prepared for Whizperz Tours bookings.

Payment options

Sandbox/UAT setup is planned for Daraja STK Push, Pesapal API 3, iPOS and Unipesa hosted checkout. These are defaults only; no merchant accounts or credentials are connected. Once your itinerary and quote are confirmed, we’ll share the available secure payment options. No payment is collected through this preview site.

M

M-Pesa Till

We plan to support local M-Pesa payments. The default plan is Daraja STK Push in sandbox. A manual Till payment can be added after the registered Till and booking-reference process are confirmed.

Safe implementation

Provider credentials belong on a secure server and should never be placed in public website code. Each connection needs server-side order creation, verified provider callbacks and a booking status update before a trip is marked paid.
