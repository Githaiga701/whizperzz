Get in touch ↗
Can you tailor a trip to our dates and interests?+

Yes. Share your dates, group size and the experiences you’re hoping for. We’ll suggest a route and pace around your trip.

What’s included in a safari package?+

Inclusions vary by itinerary. Your quote will spell out accommodation, transport, guiding, meals, park fees and activities that are included.

When is the best time to visit Kenya?+

Kenya is rewarding across the year. The right timing depends on your destinations, interests and preferred travel conditions. We can help you choose.

Can I pay with M-Pesa or card?+

We are preparing local and international options, including M-Pesa Till, IPOS Pay, Pesapal, Unipesa and cryptocurrency. We’ll confirm the available method and secure payment instructions with your booking quote.

Do you arrange airport transfers and stays?+

Yes. We can include airport transfers and handpicked stays in your plan, based on your preferences and budget.

Can children join a safari?+

Many trips can be planned for families. Tell us the ages of the children travelling so we can recommend suitable stays, activities and travel times.

What should I bring?+

We’ll share a trip-specific packing list before departure. Comfortable neutral layers, sun protection and a camera are useful starting points.
