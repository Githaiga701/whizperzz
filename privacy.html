Privacy policy.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This page is a starter privacy notice for the Whizperz Tours website. It should be reviewed and completed with the business’s actual contact details, operating practices and applicable legal requirements before publication.

Information you share

The enquiry form on this preview does not currently send or store the information entered. When the form is connected, Whizperz Tours may use details you choose to provide—such as your name, email, travel dates and trip preferences—to respond to your enquiry and prepare a travel proposal. This notice must be updated to describe the final form provider, storage and retention practices.

Translation and external content

This site loads Google Translate to offer machine translation. Google may process technical data when the translation feature is used. Destination photography is loaded from Unsplash image URLs, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Their own privacy terms apply to those services.

Payments

No payment is collected by this preview. M-Pesa Till, IPOS Pay, Pesapal, Unipesa and cryptocurrency are shown as planned payment options. Before enabling any payment service, this notice should identify the providers in use and explain what payment and transaction information they process.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit personal information through the site. When a live enquiry or booking service is connected, Whizperz Tours should provide a contact method for privacy questions, access or correction requests, and other rights available under applicable law.
