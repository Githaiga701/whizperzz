Tsavo is where you feel how big Kenya is. The park is split into East and West, with lava fields, springs and long empty tracks between them. You won't see many other vehicles.

We often use Tsavo to link a southern safari with the coast, so the drive becomes part of the trip rather than time lost on the road.
