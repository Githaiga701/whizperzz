Samburu feels different from the moment you arrive: drier, hotter and more open, with the Ewaso Ng'iro river running through it. Gerenuk, Grevy's zebra, the reticulated giraffe and the Somali ostrich all live here.

It pairs well with Laikipia, which sits on the way.
