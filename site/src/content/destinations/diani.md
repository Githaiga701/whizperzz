Diani is a long, bright beach south of Mombasa with a reef offshore that keeps the water calm. It's where many of our guests finish their trip, after the early mornings of a safari.

Snorkelling at Kisite, a dhow trip, fresh seafood and a few days of doing very little. We can arrange as much or as little as you want.
