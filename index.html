Ask us anything ↗
Can you tailor a trip to our dates and interests?+

Absolutely. Tell us when you’re travelling, who’s coming and what you’re drawn to. We’ll shape a route and pace around you.

What’s included in a safari package?+

It depends on the journey. We’ll make the details clear in your quote, including transport, accommodation, meals, park fees and activities.

When is the best time to visit Kenya?+

Kenya rewards a visit in every season. The best time depends on where you want to go and what you’d love to see, and we can help you choose.

Can I pay with M-Pesa or card?+

We’re preparing convenient local and international payment options, including M-Pesa Till, IPOS Pay, Pesapal, Unipesa and cryptocurrency. We’ll confirm the available method and payment details with your booking quote.

Do you arrange airport transfers and stays?+

Yes. We can include airport transfers and handpicked accommodation in your itinerary, based on your preferences and budget.
